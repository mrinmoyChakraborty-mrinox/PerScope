SECURITY POLICY: FAIL-CLOSED

No single model is ever trusted alone

Three independent detection signals are cross-checked before anything is redacted. If any detector reports ambiguity or low confidence, the pipeline fails closed — redacting by default rather than guessing with your private data.

Heuristic regex engines verify mathematical entropy while localized neural transformers scan for implicit identity cues. Unresolved variance triggers immediate, zero-trust token masking.
